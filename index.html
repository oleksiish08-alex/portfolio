Approach

A systems engineering approach to design

A degree in systems engineering from Dnipro University of Technology means I don't just draw interfaces, I think in constraints, states, and hand-offs. That background shapes three things in every project I take on:

How I work

Select a step to see how research turns into shipped, accessible UI.

1. Research

User research and journey mapping to understand who the product is for and where they struggle.

2. Flows & structure

Information architecture, user flows and wireframes that make the logic of the product clear before any visual polish.

3. UI & design system

Scalable design systems, component libraries and tokens that match what engineers actually build.

4. Prototype & validate

Flows, wireframes and interactive prototypes built for fast validation.

5. Hand-off

Specifications and design tokens that correspond directly to what engineers build, which reduces development time.

WCAG 2.2 checked at every step — color, contrast and flows, not a late-stage audit.
